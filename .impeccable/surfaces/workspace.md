# StudyMatch workspace

Mode: operate. Build path: code. Applies to frontend/ and the local application at /.

## THESIS
Reusable prescreen answers make research participation easier: discover a preference-ranked study, answer only missing questions, and book a real session.

## OWN-WORLD
User-pinned Linear-style workspace: neutral light rail, white canvas, crisp system sans, restrained indigo actions, compact rows with clear alignment. Academic research content supplies the detail. No cream/serif marketing hero from the rejected prototype.

## STORY
Signup chooses participant or researcher. Participants complete a 14-field prescreen with birth date and explicit yes/no answers, then see ranked studies, remaining questions and a time-grid calendar. Researchers import a document or enter study facts, check an AI-prepared preview, approve publication and manage bookings or participant review requests.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
After onboarding, navigation on the left; titled rows with compensation, duration, format, availability and screening status in the center. A single control hides the entire priorities, next-session and alert sidebar and returns that space to the list. Scores are labelled preference fit, separate from eligibility. Mobile brings navigation and the optional panel above the list. No redundant workspace banner or ranking slogan.
