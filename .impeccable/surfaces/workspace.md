# StudyMatch workspace

Mode: operate. Build path: code. Applies to frontend/ and the local application at /.

## THESIS
Reusable prescreen answers make research participation easier: discover a preference-ranked study, answer only missing questions, and book a real session.

## OWN-WORLD
User-pinned Linear-style workspace: neutral light rail, white canvas, crisp system sans, restrained indigo actions, compact rows with clear alignment. Academic research content supplies the detail. No cream/serif marketing hero from the rejected prototype.

## STORY
Signup chooses participant or researcher. Participants complete a reusable prescreen with birth date, language proficiency and explicit yes/no answers. Optional general prompts are ported from the friend's prototype; the medical section is removed. Signup includes interests and priorities in the same form. Existing accounts edit these directly and save independently. Format/location/duration/time and notification preference controls are removed. Participants filter ranked studies, answer only missing questions, read notifications and book in a time-grid calendar. Researchers import a document or enter study facts, choose topics, check an AI-prepared preview and manage publication, bookings and review requests.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
After onboarding, collapsible navigation on the left with the logo always retained; titled rows with compensation, duration, format, availability and screening status fill the working area. Format, topic and eligibility filters sit above the list. No right preference or score sidebar. Scores are labelled preference fit, separate from eligibility. Mobile navigation opens and closes explicitly. No redundant workspace banner or ranking slogan.
